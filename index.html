We believe a library is the most generous room in town.

Greenwood Public Library opened more than forty years ago with a single room and a few hundred donated books. Today it spans three floors, holds over twelve thousand titles and welcomes thousands of visitors every month.

Our six sections are arranged so that browsing feels natural. Start with a novel on the first floor, wander up to the science shelves, and end the afternoon in a sunlit corner with a design book you did not plan to open.

We are more than shelves. There are quiet study rooms for exams, a digital lab for coding and research, and a children's corner where story time turns into the best part of the week.

Membership is free for everyone in Greenwood, and our events are open to all. Bring a friend, bring your questions, or simply bring yourself, because there is always a seat waiting.
